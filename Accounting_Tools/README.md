# EasyParcel Accounting Tool — Guides

The Accounting Tool sends your EasyParcel costs, your store's sales and their payouts straight into your accounting software. It belongs to your **organization** in EasyAccount: open it from the **Products** menu (the grid icon) and choose **Accounting**.

| Guide | Read it when |
|---|---|
| [SQL Account](SQLAccountGuideline.md) | You use SQL Account and want EasyParcel to send documents into it |
| [AutoCount Cloud](AutoCountCloudGuideline.md) | You use AutoCount Cloud Accounting and want EasyParcel to send documents into it |
| [Payouts](Payouts.md) | You send your sales, and want to record what Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop or Shopify paid you |
| [Export to a file](Export.md) | You want a file to import yourself, or to hand to your accountant, with no connection |

All guides work for Malaysia (MYR) and Singapore (SGD) accounts.
